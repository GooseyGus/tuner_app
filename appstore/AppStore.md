## Description [4000]

A chromatic tuner that keeps everything on one screen. No ads, no accounts, no settings pages: open it and play.

ANY NOTE, ANY INSTRUMENT
Guitar, bass, ukulele, violin, cello, voice, wind and brass. It hears every note from a piano's lowest A to its highest C and names it with its octave, like A4.

ACCURATE TO ABOUT A CENT
From low E (E2) up to C7. It uses the YIN pitch algorithm, which isn't fooled by overtones, so a guitar's low E reads as E2, not the octave above.

SEE IT AT A GLANCE
The whole screen turns green when you're in tune and red when you're flat or sharp. A cents meter shows exactly how far off you are.

WATCH YOUR PITCH MOVE
A pitch trail draws the last six seconds of your note down the screen, flat to the left and sharp to the right, so you can see a note drift, wobble or settle. Great for singers and string players working on intonation.

MADE TO LOOK AT
Eight colour palettes, each with its own green and red, and a layout made for iPhone and iPad, in portrait and landscape.

PRIVATE
The microphone is only used while the app is on screen. Nothing is recorded or leaves your device.
