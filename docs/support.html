The tuner isn't showing anything

Check the app has microphone access: Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone › Tuner. Then play a single, steady note close to the phone.

What can I tune?

Anything that plays a note: guitar, bass, ukulele, violin, voice, wind and brass instruments. It's accurate to about a cent from low E (E2) up to C7.

What do the colours mean?

The screen turns green when you're within 10 cents of the note, and red when you're flat or sharp. The meter shows how far off you are, and the trail below shows the last few seconds, flat to the left and sharp to the right.

How do I change the colours?

Tap the palette button in the top right to cycle through the eight palettes.
