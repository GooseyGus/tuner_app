Tuner Perfect

Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Tuner Perfect collects no data. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app never connects to the internet.

The microphone

The tuner needs the microphone to hear which note you're playing. It listens only while the app is open on your screen. The sound is analysed live, on your device, and immediately discarded: it is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your chosen colour palette is saved on your device so the app opens the way you left it. That's the only thing the app stores.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
